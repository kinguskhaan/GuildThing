Cancelling an event (via "Cancel group" in Discord or the website) no longer deletes its post and thread — the post is edited to show "❌ Cancelled" with its sign-up/vote buttons removed, and the thread is locked and archived instead of deleted. This leaves a browsable archive of past events (still listed on the website's Events page too) instead of them just vanishing. A mod can still manually unarchive/delete a thread themselves if they want it gone for good.
