Raid comp tool: officer-only composition builder at /guilds/[slug]/admin/raid-comp. Snap roster members into a dynamic block-wall grid (groups view) or role buckets (tanks/healers/ranged/melee), filter the roster by class and guild rank, set specs manually or auto-sync them from Battle.net, and watch per-expansion buff/debuff coverage fill in live. Guilds get an expansion setting (Classic–MoP) driving class/spec/buff catalogs and onboarding class lists.
